The Real Math

100 units/month at Bangtao is the first month's velocity, not a ceiling — and it's a strong signal, not the global baseline either. Below is a conservative floor for reaching $1M+ ARR, not a forecast of the top end. The global soap category alone is growing ~7–9% a year, and a single-SKU competitor like Defense Soap is independently estimated at $10M–$100M+ in annual revenue. A multi-line, multi-channel brand has materially more room above this floor.

Two rules, whatever the metric

Whatever milestones we agree on: 1) they must be something both sides can check without argument, and 2) 15% is the ceiling, full stop — not a starting point for further negotiation. Maximum possible stake for Bangtao, all-in: 35%.
