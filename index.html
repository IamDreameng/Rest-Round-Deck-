Objective Unit Economics

The Real Math

Moving 100 bottles in 30 days at Bangtao is a powerful baseline, but Bangtao is a high-traffic anomaly. To prove a rigorous path to a $1M+ ARR, we must discount that velocity globally and factor in the necessity of multi-product line expansion and realistic operating expenses (OPEX).

The Discounted Scale Model

Bangtao Peak Velocity: 100 units / product / mo
Discounted Global Node Avg: 30 units / product / mo
Average Wholesale Unit Yield: $12.00
Gross Margin Assumptions: DTC (75%) | B2B (45%)
Operating Overhead (OPEX+CAC): -30% of Total Revenue
* We do not offer the fund a cut of Gross Margin. The 20% Yield is calculated on true Net Profit (EBITDA) after deducting a hard 30% OPEX constraint to cover logistics, marketing, and team overhead.

Product Expansion is Mandatory

A gym moving 30 Sprays is only $360/mo. But a gym moving 30 units across 5 product lines (Spray, Soap, Wipes, Pouches, Sheets) generates $1,800/mo. This is how we hit scale.
